02 — Replay · proof of the day

This screen is the whole pitch. A real storm, our probability climbing day by day, and the exact gap between our threshold and the official watch — stated in hours, not marketing language.

It's the press demo, the trust-builder, the onboarding tutorial, and the thing that keeps the app alive in the off-season. Timeline figures shown are placeholders until we run the real hindcasts.

03 — Tracker · the ensemble fan

Unchanged, and still the signature visual. Every faint line is one WeatherNext 2 scenario; tracks that miss are drawn too, because showing the misses is what makes the hits believable.

New: a Rain layer for the flooding-rain risk that does most of the damage in Australian cyclones, and a reverse scrub to watch the fan tighten run over run.

Windward is a preparedness layer, not a warning service. The extra lead time is an average improvement, not a guarantee on every storm — the Replay and calibration screens show performance case by case. Official warnings, watches and advice come from the Bureau of Meteorology and state emergency services.
